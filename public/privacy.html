RKMAerial Privacy Policy

RKMAerial is a small travel-fare service run by Rafiq Mateen. The "Fare Gmail" tool is used only by RKMAerial, on its own account (rkmaerial@gmail.com), to send fare reports to clients who asked for them and to read replies about those reports. It does not collect data about anyone else, does not sell or share data, and does not show ads. Trip request details are kept only to prepare the report. To ask about or remove your details, write to rkmaerial@gmail.com.
